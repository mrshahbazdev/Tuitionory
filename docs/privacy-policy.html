Tuitionory — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Contact: mrshahbaznns@gmail.com

What Tuitionory stores

Tuitionory keeps school records you enter: students' names, photos, guardian contact details, class enrolments, fee plans, challans and payment receipts. Some of this data relates to children.

Where the data lives

All records are stored only on the computer running Tuitionory, in a locally encrypted (SQLCipher) database whose key is protected by the operating system's credential store. The app makes no internet requests — nothing is uploaded to us or to any third party, and there are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Who controls the data

The school is the data controller. The publisher of Tuitionory has no access to your records. The school is responsible for obtaining any required consent from parents or guardians, for access control (user PINs and roles are provided in the app), and for backups.

Optional LAN sharing

A "share on WiFi" mode lets another computer on the same local network open the app in a browser. It is off by default, requires a randomly generated access code, and never exposes data beyond the local network. No cloud relay is used.

Backups

Manual and scheduled backups are written only to folders you choose (e.g. a USB drive). Their handling is the school's responsibility.

Changes

This policy may be updated with app releases; the latest version ships inside the app and on this page.
